Why it fits plumbing here

Plumbing work comes in two kinds. Emergencies cannot wait, and the homeowner books whoever answers first. Planned jobs like a new water heater, a repipe or a filtration system are bigger tickets, and homeowners take their time choosing.

Water quality has been a local concern ever since GenX was found in the Cape Fear River, so whole-home filtration gets real interest here. Ads are how homeowners find out you install it.

What we advertise

We focus on the planned, higher-ticket work: tank and tankless water heater replacements, whole-home filtration, repipes and drain cleaning. A simple new-customer offer on a common job also works well for getting a first visit.

The ads use your own photos, your own trucks and your own team. We only advertise services you actually provide.

What happens to every lead

Within 60 seconds of filling out the form, the homeowner gets a text from your business. It asks what is going on, whether it is urgent or planned, and where the home is.

Then it offers open times and books the visit on your calendar. You see their answers first, so you know what to bring and which jobs need you today.

Honest about emergencies

Someone with a burst pipe at 2 a.m. usually picks up the phone or searches Google, not Facebook. We will not tell you ads replace that.

What ads do is make you the plumber they already know when something breaks, and bring in the planned jobs that fill your week. When an urgent lead does come through, the 60-second text means you are the first one to answer. See plans and pricing.

Questions plumbers ask

Do Facebook ads work for plumbers?

They work best for planned jobs like water heaters, filtration and repipes, and for keeping your name in front of local homeowners. They are not the main way people find an emergency plumber at night, and we will tell you that up front.

Can the ads reach only my service area?

Yes. Meta lets us target by city, ZIP code or a set radius, so your ads only show to homeowners in the areas you actually serve.

How much should I spend on ads?

You set the ad budget and pay Meta directly from your own account, so you can raise, lower or pause it at any time. On the strategy call we look at your service area and what a job is worth to you, then suggest a starting budget that makes sense.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $1,000 a month plus $75 per booked appointment, with a $500 one-time setup. Ad spend is separate and goes straight from you to Meta. Every plan comes with one guarantee: we keep working for free until you get your first 5 qualified leads.
