Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, start a chat on this site, or fill out one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram, we collect the information you provide: your name, business name, type of business, phone number, and email address, along with the messages you send us. If you accept cookies, we also collect standard website usage information through cookies and similar technologies, such as the pages you visit and how you arrived at our site.

How we use your information

We use your contact information to respond to your inquiry, discuss our services with you, and follow up about working together. We use website usage data to understand how visitors use our site and to measure and improve our advertising.

Meta Pixel and advertising

If you accept cookies, this website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc., to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram. The Pixel collects information such as pages visited and actions taken on our site. When you submit our contact form or start a chat, the Pixel records that a lead was submitted so we can measure which ads work. When you start a chat, it may also receive a scrambled (hashed) version of your email address and name so Meta can match the lead to our ads. We do not send your phone number or text messaging consent to Meta. If you decline cookies, the Pixel does not load. You can learn more about how Meta handles this data in Meta's Privacy Policy, and you can adjust your ad preferences in your Facebook settings.

Cookies

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept cookies. Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel do not load, and set no cookies, unless you tap Accept. If you tap Decline or make no choice, they stay off. We remember your choice in your browser for 12 months, and you can change it at any time with Cookie choices, also at the bottom of every page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as Decline.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies to tell visits apart and does not receive your name, phone number or email from our forms, and the Meta Pixel works as described above. Our contact forms, chat and scheduling are provided by GoHighLevel and are needed for those features to work. You can also delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Declining cookies will not prevent you from using this site or contacting us.

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted services to operate this website: GoHighLevel (LeadConnector) runs our contact form, chat, text messaging and scheduling, GitHub Pages hosts this website, Google Analytics shows us which pages are visited and how visitors found the site, and Meta provides our advertising measurement as described above. Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts, we use it only to reply to your inquiry, qualify your request and schedule appointments. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Our messaging provider processes your number only to deliver messages on our behalf.

Some replies to texts and website chat are written by an automated AI assistant so you get an answer quickly. Website chats and text conversations are recorded and stored in our customer system, provided by GoHighLevel, and reviewed by us. You can ask to talk to a person at any time.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and maintain our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.
