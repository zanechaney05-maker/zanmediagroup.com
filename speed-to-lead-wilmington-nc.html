Why speed matters

A homeowner with a leak, a dead AC unit or a backed-up drain usually contacts more than one company. The first one to answer is the one they talk to first, and often the one they hire.

You are on a roof, under a house or driving between jobs. You cannot pick up every call or answer every form the minute it comes in. The system answers for you, so the lead is already talking to your business while you finish the job in front of you.

What happens to every lead

Within 60 seconds, the homeowner gets a text from your business, day or night. It asks the questions you would ask: what they need, where the home is, whether they own it, and how soon they want it done.

Once they answer, it offers open times and books the appointment straight onto your calendar. You get their name, number, what they need and when you are meeting them, before you ever pick up the phone.

If they do not reply, it follows up with up to two more texts, timed so they do not land in the middle of the night.

What it is built on

Texts go out from a business number registered for texting, with the opt-out wording carriers require, so your messages get delivered instead of filtered.

Every conversation lands in one inbox you can open on your phone. You can read any thread and take over whenever you want.

Which plans include it

Lead follow-up and automatic booking come with the Growth and Partner plans. On the Starter plan, leads come straight to you with their name, number and what they need, and you follow up yourself. See plans and pricing.

Questions contractors ask

How fast does the first text go out?

Within 60 seconds of the lead coming in, day or night, weekends included.

What if they do not reply?

It follows up with up to two more texts, timed so they do not land in the middle of the night. If they still do not answer, the lead stays on your list so you can call them yourself.

Can I see the conversations and step in?

Yes. Every text thread is in one inbox you can open on your phone. You can read what was said and take over a conversation at any time.

Does it work with leads from my website?

Yes. Leads from your Facebook and Instagram ads come in automatically, and we can connect your website form the same way.

How much does it cost?
