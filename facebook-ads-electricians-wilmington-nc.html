01

Why it fits electrical work here

Atlantic hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30, and every long outage gets homeowners thinking about a whole-home generator. That is a big planned purchase, and they look at a few electricians before they choose.

Many older homes around Wilmington are due for a panel upgrade, and fast-growing areas like Leland and Hampstead bring steady demand for EV chargers and new circuits. These are jobs people plan for, which is where ads do their best work.

02

What we advertise

We build campaigns around your higher-ticket planned work: whole-home generators, panel upgrades, EV charger installs, surge protection and lighting. If you offer financing on generators, we lead with that.

The ads use your own job photos and your own team. We only advertise services you actually provide.

03

What happens to every lead

Within 60 seconds of filling out the form, the homeowner gets a text from your business. It asks what kind of job it is, how soon they want it done and where the home is.

Then it offers open times and books the estimate on your calendar. You see their answers first, so you can bring the right information to the visit.

04

Timing it around storm season

Interest in generators jumps right before and right after a storm, which is also when every electrician in the area is booked. We start your generator campaigns in spring, so the estimates and installs happen before the season peaks.

The rest of the year we rotate through panel upgrades, EV chargers and whatever keeps your crews busiest. See plans and pricing.
