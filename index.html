Questions contractors ask

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $1,000 a month plus $75 per booked appointment, with a $500 one-time setup. Ad spend is separate and goes straight from you to Meta.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, from your own ad account. We never mark it up, so you see exactly where every dollar goes.

How fast do you contact new leads?

Within 60 seconds, by text, day or night. The system qualifies them and books the appointment on your calendar.

How long is the contract?

90 days to start, then month-to-month with 30 days notice.

What if it doesn't work?

We keep working for free until you get your first 5 qualified leads.

What trades do you work with?

Home-service contractors: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, painting, remodeling, and cleaning or pressure washing.
